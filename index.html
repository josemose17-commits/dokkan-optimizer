Dokkan build finder

Enter the kit for one battle situation, then choose the orbs you can use. This is a manual scenario comparison; it does not automatically read or simulate the whole kit. The tool tries every orb combination in the three slots and every way to place your Hidden Potential levels. It ranks builds by how much they widen the team’s margin in a fight: more damage ends it sooner, and dodges, Type DEF and healing make the team’s HP last longer.

Inputs from the previous version of this tool were found in this browser. That version assumed every two-skill orb gives +5 to each skill and that bronze orbs carry skills. Real orbs are closer to: bronze = stats only (up to ATK +800), one-skill orbs up to 7, two-skill silver orbs 4 + 4.

Your old data stays in the browser either way. Nothing is deleted.

5. Which slot?

More options

Crits ignore enemy DEF, so a higher value makes Crit worth more. 0 ignores DEF.

Sizes flat ATK on orbs: +800 on a 20,000 ATK card is +4%.

Recovery Boost heals more when it picks up Ki Spheres of its own Type. Its per-level amount isn’t verified, so estimate it; it scales with its level. Leave 0 to ignore Recovery.

Game values used
  • Per level: Additional +2% (a separate check per attack before it; half of them are Supers), Crit +2%, Evasion +1%, Super Attack Boost +0.05, Type ATK +0.05, Type DEF −0.01 (gamerch, DokkanStats).
  • Normal hits: neutral 1×, neutral Super vs Extreme 1.15×, a Type it beats 1.25× (same class) or 1.5× (Super vs Extreme) plus Type ATK, a Type that beats it 0.9× or 1.0× (kamigame).
  • Crits ignore enemy DEF and hit as super effective: 1.5 × 1.25 = 1.875, or (1.5 + Type ATK) × 1.25 against a Type it beats.
  • Damage taken from a Type it beats: 0.9 (same class) or 1.0 (Super vs Extreme), minus Type DEF, then halved by the natural guard. Guarding gives the same without Type DEF. From a Type that beats it: 1.25× or 1.5×. Neutral Super vs Extreme: 1.15× (DokkanStats, kamigame).
  • Counters hit for ATK × the counter multiplier × the Type multiplier.
  • Not verified: the 15-level cap per skill and which skill the star node gives each Type, so you set both.

Top builds

  • Score: a fight is treated as a race between the team’s damage and its HP. Team margin = (1 + damage share × damage gain) ÷ (1 − cut in team HP loss) − 1. So 1% more team damage is worth the same as 1% less team HP lost per turn.
  • Damage is expected damage per turn compared with no Hidden Potential skills and no orbs, averaged over forms by their share of turns. Team HP loss counts the hits this unit takes against everything the team takes.
  • Main Super: card Ki multiplier at the Ki you set, blended between the 12 Ki and 24 Ki multipliers, with the 18 Ki Super from 18 Ki. Extra Supers from the kit and Hidden Potential additionals use the 12 Ki Super and the 12 Ki multiplier, as on DokkanStats. Super Attack Boost and stacked ATK add to the Super multiplier.
  • Counters, on-hit stacks and on-evade gains are set per form as a share of its own attacks’ damage, so they grow with its damage. A dodge avoids the hit’s damage and swaps what a received hit is worth for what a dodge is worth.
  • Not modelled: HP, ATK and DEF stat nodes beyond the card ATK you enter, which exact skills each board route offers, leader skills, links (only the slot advice uses them), ATK that keeps stacking within the turn, enemy DEF on incoming hits.
