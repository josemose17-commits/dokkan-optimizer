Add a row for each defensive, offensive or team effect, with where it's written and the condition on the card. A unit with damage reduction at the start of turn and a guard after its Super gets two rows. Plain stat boosts with no condition can be skipped unless they're defensive.

Which "Turns on" to pick
  • Start of turn: no condition, "at the start of turn", "when HP is X% or more", category or Ki-sphere conditions.
  • When it's hit, every hit: "when receiving an attack" (on for the first hit too).
  • After it's hit: "after receiving an attack", "for every attack received" (off until the first hit).
  • After it evades: "after evading an attack", "for every attack evaded".
  • When it attacks: "when attacking", "when performing a Super Attack", or the Super's own "raises DEF".
  • After its Super: "after performing a Super Attack", "for every Super performed within the turn".
  • After allies attack: needs teammates to attack before it, e.g. "for every ally who attacked before this character".
  • From: where the line is written. Super and Ultra Super effects happen when it attacks; Super Attack effects repeat on every additional Super, Ultra Super effects don't.
  • Lasts: "within the turn" or no duration = this turn only. "For 2 turns", "for 3 turns" and so on. Stacks with no end = rest of the battle. That includes a Super that "raises ATK" or "raises ATK & DEF" with no "for X turns" (each Super adds it again for good), and passive stacks like "for every Super performed" without "within the turn", even if capped "up to X%".
  • Amount: the % on the card, e.g. "damage reduction rate 40%" = 40, "great chance of evading" = its footnote %. Leave blank if you don't know; a typical value is used.
  • Needs an HP condition: "when HP is 50% or less" and similar. It counts for a bit less, since it isn't always on.
  • Needs a certain ally in the same turn: "when there is another [Category] ally attacking in the same turn". This decides its rotation partner, not its slot.
  • Lowers enemy ATK: "lowers ATK" on its Super or passive. It protects the character itself after it lands and every teammate hit after that, so from slot 1 it covers the most.
  • Needs a later turn: "starting from the 4th turn", effects after a transformation. Active Skill effects count as later automatically.

    How it decides

    Slot

    • In slot 1 the enemy can attack before and after the character attacks. In slots 2 and 3 the character always attacks before it is hit.
    • For each slot it checks which of the effects you listed are on at the moment it can be hit: before its attack (slot 1 only) and after it. Guard counts most, then damage reduction and evasion, then DEF boosts. Effects that only start "after being hit" count for half before its attack.
    • Defence from its Super that lasts 3 turns or more is still up when a rotation unit comes back, so from its 2nd appearance it also protects in slot 1 before it attacks.
    • Effects that need enemy attacks (counters, boosts after being hit) favour slot 1. Stuns, seals, stat-lowering and ally boosts that come from attacking favour slot 1, then slot 2. Effects that need allies to attack first favour slot 3. Effects written for a position point straight to that slot.
    • Slots 1 and 2 are the rotation, so they come back every other turn. Slot 3 is the floater, fine for characters whose value starts later.

    Build

    • Every board choice and orb combination is tried, and each one is scored against the same character with no Hidden Potential skills, using the card's own Supers and chances.
    • Additional: 2% per level, rolled on every attack and counter, at most one per turn, a Super half the time, using the 12 Ki Super. Crit: 2% per level, rolls separately from the kit's crit, crits treated as 1.875× damage. Evasion: 1% per level, rolls separately. SA Boost: adds 0.05 per level to the Super multiplier.
    • Forms each get their own attack numbers. A build is scored in each form against that form without Hidden Potential, then the scores are blended by time spent in each form.
    • Ki: an LR at 24 Ki uses a 2.0 Ki multiplier, at 18–23 about 1.8, and below 18 it uses its 12 Ki Super at about 1.6. Counters add damage at the counter strength you enter, assumed to use the 12 Ki multiplier and be able to crit; SA Boost doesn't apply to them. Both are estimates.
    • Type ATK adds 0.05 per level to the Type-advantage multiplier and Type DEF takes 0.01 per level off damage from Types you beat. Type ATK counts when you tick "effective against all Types" or "mostly used against a Type it beats"; Type DEF only with the second. Recovery heals too little to change a build and isn't scored.
