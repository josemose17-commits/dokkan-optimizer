Dokkan build finder

Describe the character, its forms and the orbs you can use. The tool tries every orb combination in the three slots and every way to place your Hidden Potential levels. It ranks builds by how much they widen the team’s margin in a fight: more damage ends it sooner, and dodges, Type DEF and healing make the team’s HP last longer.

Inputs from the previous version of this tool were found in this browser. That version assumed every two-skill orb gives +5 to each skill and that bronze orbs carry skills. Real orbs are closer to: bronze = stats only (up to ATK +800), one-skill orbs up to 7, two-skill silver orbs 4 + 4.

Your old data stays in the browser either way. Nothing is deleted.

Filling in by hand? Where each line of the kit goes
  • Skip: start-of-turn “ATK +X%” and “DEF +X%”, per Ki Sphere, per ally, leader skills and links. They raise every attack alike, so they don’t change which build wins.
  • Super Attack effects: “raises ATK for 1 turn”, “raises ATK” for the battle or several turns, “lowers DEF”, Supers that hit all enemies.
  • Additional attacks: “launches an additional Super Attack” or attack, and their chances.
  • Crit, evasion and Type: “performs a critical hit”, “evades”, “attacks effective against all Types”.
  • ATK boosts when it attacks: “when performing a Super Attack”, “with each attack performed”, “with each attack received”.
  • When it’s attacked: counters, and “ATK +X%” after receiving or evading an attack.
  • The card: “damage received −X%” and guard. Below the forms: Active Skill and Finish Skill attacks.
  • A new form: transformations, Exchange, Giant or Rage forms, Standby. Set each form’s share of turns.
  • Domains: enter their effects where they fit (damage reduction, enemy DEF, ATK on Supers).
  • Not counted: revival, healing other than Recovery Boost, enemy ATK down, stun and seal. For stun and seal, lower the attacks it receives instead.
Active or Finish Skill attacks

Spread over the turns it attacks in the fight (The fight, More about the fight). It counts as a Super at its top Ki multiplier, so SA Boost, Crit and Type ATK count for it (Dokkan wiki).

5. Which slot?

More options

Sizes flat ATK on orbs: +800 on a 20,000 ATK card is +4%.

Recovery Boost heals 0.0015 × its level × DEF for each Ki Sphere of its Type (Dokkan wiki). With the enemy’s ATK, its DEF and the Ki Spheres the tool works it out; otherwise estimate it. Leave 0 to ignore Recovery.

Game values used
  • Per level: Additional +2% (a separate check per attack before it; half of them are Supers), Crit +2%, Evasion +1%, Super Attack Boost +0.05, Type ATK +0.05, Type DEF −0.01 (gamerch, DokkanStats).
  • Normal hits: neutral 1×, neutral Super vs Extreme 1.15×, a Type it beats 1.25× (same class) or 1.5× (Super vs Extreme) plus Type ATK, a Type that beats it 0.9× or 1.0× (kamigame).
  • Crits ignore enemy DEF and hit as super effective: 1.5 × 1.25 = 1.875, or (1.5 + Type ATK) × 1.25 against a Type it beats.
  • Damage taken from a Type it beats: 0.9 (same class) or 1.0 (Super vs Extreme), minus Type DEF, then halved by the natural guard. Guarding gives the same without Type DEF. From a Type that beats it: 1.25× or 1.5×. Neutral Super vs Extreme: 1.15× (DokkanStats, kamigame).
  • Counters hit for ATK × (1 + ATK raised by earlier Supers) × the counter multiplier × the Type multiplier, and still happen when it evades (Dokkan wiki).
  • Recovery Boost: (0.03 + 0.0015 × level) × DEF × Ki Spheres of its Type (Dokkan wiki).
  • Attack order: start-of-turn boosts, then links and Ki, then on-attack boosts, then the Super multiplier with Super ATK raises, then Type and crit (Dokkan wiki). “Effective against all Types” attacks with Type advantage.
  • Not verified: the 15-level cap per skill and which skill the star node gives each Type, so you set both; whether counters can crit (counted: yes), use the Ki multiplier (counted: no) or get “ATK when attacking” (counted: yes); whether Active Skill attacks can crit (counted: yes); where the Hidden Potential additional falls in the turn (counted: last); that a lowered DEF from its previous attacking turn is still up.

Best build

Top builds

  • Score: a fight is treated as a race between the team’s damage and its HP. Team margin = (1 + damage share × damage gain) ÷ (1 − cut in team HP loss) − 1. So 1% more team damage is worth the same as 1% less team HP lost per turn.
  • Damage is expected damage per turn compared with no Hidden Potential skills and no orbs, averaged over forms by their share of turns. Team HP loss counts the hits this unit takes against everything the team takes.
  • Main Super: card Ki multiplier at the Ki you set, blended between the 12 Ki and 24 Ki multipliers, with the 18 Ki Super from 18 Ki. Extra Supers from the kit and Hidden Potential additionals use the 12 Ki Super and the 12 Ki multiplier, as on DokkanStats. Super Attack Boost and stacked ATK add to the Super multiplier.
  • A turn is its main Super, the kit’s extra attacks, then the Hidden Potential additional, averaged over the turns it attacks. ATK raised by a Super counts for that Super and the attacks after it; on-attack boosts add up, then multiply.
  • Counters hit for ATK × (1 + raised ATK) × the counter multiplier. Crit and Type ATK count for them, SA Boost and Additional don’t, and each counter is a Hidden Potential additional check. It counters whether it’s hit or evades. ATK gained per hit before it attacks is lost when it dodges.
  • Not modelled: HP stat nodes, which exact skills each board route offers, leader skills and links (they raise every attack alike; only the slot advice uses links), revival, healing other than Recovery Boost, stun, seal and enemy ATK down.
